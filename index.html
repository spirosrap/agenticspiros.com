Working principles

Keep the machinery visible.

The interesting part is not the amount of automation. It is whether the state, evidence, and failure path remain understandable after the novelty wears off.

  1. 01 Show what is true.

    Logs, receipts, history, and clear status beat optimistic dashboards.

  2. 02 Earn the abstraction.

    Start small, repeat the workflow, and add structure only where use proves it belongs.

  3. 03 Bound the agent.

    AI is useful when evidence, permissions, and the irreversible step are explicit.

Working style

The logic stays visible.

Most projects begin as one focused tool and grow only after repeated use. That keeps the software explainable, portable, and easier to repair when the real environment disagrees with the plan.
