Working principles

Make the machinery understandable.

Automation is only useful when the evidence, permissions, and failure path remain legible after the novelty wears off.

  1. 01

    Show what is true.

    Receipts, history, and clear status beat optimistic dashboards.

  2. 02

    Earn the abstraction.

    Start small, repeat the workflow, and structure only where use proves it belongs.

  3. 03

    Bound the agent.

    Make evidence, permissions, and every irreversible step explicit.
