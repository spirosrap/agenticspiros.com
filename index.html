01 / Consumer web product

Public app · v0.38.2

Καλάθι Τιμών

A shopping list becomes a better buying decision.

Compare the cost of your whole basket across Greek supermarkets. Choose up to four stops, explore price changes, and see when an extra trip is worth the saving.

Open the app ↗Source ↗
Latest: a warmer interface, clearer prices

The app moved to a warmer palette with Greek-native Commissioner type on 29 September 2026. Package cost and price per kilogram or litre are clearly distinguished, and six consistent full downloads over at least six hours can establish a changed retailer baseline.

The public health page explains catalogue coverage; price history makes changes inspectable and exportable.

02 / Native operations software

Public macOS · v1.67

Fleetlight

Know what needs attention. Verify what changed.

A native Mac app for monitoring and maintaining Macs and Linux machines, with an Android companion. It brings health checks, incident history, and confirmed updates into one place.

macOS source ↗Android source ↗
Latest: Arch support and smarter tool updates

Package detection now understands pacman-based Linux systems. Tool updates recognize installations managed by mise and verify the active version afterward.

Fresh checks can clear an old failure once the machine is confirmed current. Public macOS 1.67 and Android 1.15.6 were released together; the new updater behavior runs on macOS.

03 / Self-hosted media

Android · v0.22.3

Plex Open

Media that keeps working when the connection changes.

A web interface and native Android client for a self-hosted media library. Offline downloads keep video, artwork, and subtitles together, with visible storage use and confirmed removal.

Android source ↗Web source ↗
Latest: offline subtitles and clearer sign-in recovery

Saved titles can acquire additional subtitle tracks while connected and retain them for offline playback. An expired session now leads to sign-in with a clear explanation.

Android 0.22.3 also dismisses the keyboard after a search, keeping the results ready to browse. The web app remains on 0.26.0 with its browser download manager.
