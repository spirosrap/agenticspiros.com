Operating rules

Keep the irreversible step obvious.

Automation earns trust when the evidence, permissions, and failure path remain legible after the novelty wears off.

  1. 01

    Observe before changing.

    Read the real state, collect evidence, and only then choose an action.

  2. 02

    Make recovery ordinary.

    History, receipts, and bounded retries turn failures into routine operating states.

  3. 03

    Verify the result.

    A completed command is not the same as a completed outcome.
